# **刘瑾 (Jin Liu)**

**Jin Liu (刘瑾)** is a student of Prof. [Xi Ning](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%E4%B9%A0%E5%AE%81+%E8%A5%BF%E5%AE%89%E7%94%B5%E5%AD%90%E7%A7%91%E6%8A%80%E5%A4%A7%E5%AD%A6) and Prof. [Yinbin Miao](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%E8%8B%97%E9%93%B6%E6%96%8C+%E8%A5%BF%E5%AE%89%E7%94%B5%E5%AD%90%E7%A7%91%E6%8A%80%E5%A4%A7%E5%AD%A6), at **Xidian University**, China. Her research focuses on **privacy-preserving machine learning**, with work on **federated learning**, **differential privacy**, and **optimization for large models**.

**Advisors:** [习宁老师 Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%E4%B9%A0%E5%AE%81+%E8%A5%BF%E5%AE%89%E7%94%B5%E5%AD%90%E7%A7%91%E6%8A%80%E5%A4%A7%E5%AD%A6) · [苗银斌老师 Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=%E8%8B%97%E9%93%B6%E6%96%8C+%E8%A5%BF%E5%AE%89%E7%94%B5%E5%AD%90%E7%A7%91%E6%8A%80%E5%A4%A7%E5%AD%A6)

**交流经历：** 目前在 **Nanyang Technological University (NTU)** 与 [Dengwei 老师](https://www.google.com/search?q=site%3Antu.edu.sg+Dengwei+personal+homepage) 进行 CSC 交流。

**家庭信息：** 丈夫为 [刘俊康（Junkang Liu）](https://junkangliu0.github.io/)，个人主页见 [Junkang Liu Homepage](https://junkangliu0.github.io/)，Google Scholar：[个人学术主页](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=N7pJWIoAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN)。
